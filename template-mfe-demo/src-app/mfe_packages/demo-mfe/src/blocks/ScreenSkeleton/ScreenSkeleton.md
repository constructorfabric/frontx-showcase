# ScreenSkeleton

The placeholder of a whole screen while its strings load.

## When to use

- Inside a busy ScreenFrame, on the Hello World, Profile and Current Theme screens.

## When not to use

- A loading section of a loaded screen: use SkeletonCard.

## Props

None.

## Parts and layout

A title placeholder (`--control-height-lg` high, 16rem wide, exemptions ledger) and a line over a SkeletonCard.

## Replaces

The strings-loading branches of the three screens.
