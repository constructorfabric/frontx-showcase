# PreviewStage

The kit's `Card` around a working example; an example wider than the page column scrolls inside the card.

## When to use

The Preview tab of a component page; inside `FoundationStage` on a foundation page.

## Props

| Prop | Content |
|------|---------|
| `children` | The example or the foundation page |

## Parts and roles

The kit's `Card` and `CardContent` (16px between the example's parts). A carousel example gets a definite width, and on a phone room for its outside arrows.

## Reproduces

The V2 showcase's component stage.
