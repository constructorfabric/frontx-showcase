# PagePlaceholder

The silhouette of a page, or of a part of one, while it loads: the kit's `Skeleton` across the page column, 7.5rem high.

## When to use

While the screen's strings, an example, its source or the registered screens load.

## Props

| Prop | Content |
|------|---------|
| `label` | Names what is loading: the skeleton becomes a `status` named by it |

## Reproduces

No V2 counterpart: V2 renders its pages statically.
