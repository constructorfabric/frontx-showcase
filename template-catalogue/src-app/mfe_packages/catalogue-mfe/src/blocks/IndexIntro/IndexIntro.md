# IndexIntro

The catalogue index's intro row: a short sentence in a fixed column on the left and the search field across the rest; stacked on a phone.

## When to use

Once, under the index's `PageHeader`.

## Props

| Prop | Content |
|------|---------|
| `intro` | The sentence |
| `search` | The search field (the kit's `Input` with `type="search"`) |

## Parts and roles

The sentence is kit-candidate `Text` in the muted tone.

## Reproduces

The V2 showcase overview's intro row: the sentence 266px wide, the search 24px to its right, 32px above the family cards (22px on a phone).
