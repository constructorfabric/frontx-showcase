# ListCardGrid

The index's family cards in two columns, each card as tall as its own rows; one column on a phone.

## When to use

Once, on the index, around one `ListCard` per family.

## Props

| Prop | Content |
|------|---------|
| `label` | The name of the `role="group"` that holds the cards |
| `children` | The cards |

## Reproduces

The V2 showcase overview's family grid: two columns 36px apart, rows 26px apart (16px apart in one column on a phone).
